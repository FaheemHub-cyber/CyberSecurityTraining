Organization Common Security Policies

Core security and compliance policies applicable across all departments and roles within our organization.

1. Master Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

This policy governs the acceptable use of all corporate technology resources, hardware, cloud services, and network infrastructure.

  • Ownership: All devices, email accounts, and data generated on corporate systems remain sole property of the organization.
  • Prohibited Conduct: Torrenting, installing unsanctioned software (Shadow IT), attempting privilege escalation, or bypassing network controls.
  • Monitoring & Privacy: Corporate systems are continuously monitored for security events; employees should have no expectation of privacy on work-issued assets.

2. Enterprise Data Classification Standard

Information assets must be categorized into four tiers with specific handling requirements:

  • Public: Marketing collateral, press releases, public website text. Free external distribution allowed.
  • Internal: Intranet wikis, organizational charts, internal policies. Restricted to employees; non-public.
  • Confidential: Financial statements, customer contracts, roadmap specs. Requires NDA or management approval to share.
  • Restricted: PII, credit cards, credentials, API keys, source code. Highest protection level; encrypted at rest & in transit.

3. Enterprise Identity & Password Policy

Mandatory standards for authentication and access control.

  • Passphrase Complexity: Passwords must be at least 16 characters long or consist of 4 random words generated via password manager.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): MFA is mandatory on every internal portal and cloud service without exception.
  • Zero Credential Sharing: Individual user accounts must never be shared among team members.

4. Remote & Mobile Work Policy

Ensuring security across hybrid work environments.

  • Corporate VPN mandatory when connecting via public Wi-Fi networks.
  • Physical screen privacy filters required in public travel environments.
  • Devices must enforce automatic lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.

5. Incident Response & Reporting Policy

Framework for identifying and reporting potential security breaches.

  • Reporting Channels: Email security@company-securehub.com or call 24/7 Hotline +1-800-555-SECURE.
  • No-Blame Culture: Employees who report accidental clicks or security errors immediately will not face punitive action.
